CustomerExcuse me. Could I tell you something about my food?
WaiterOf course. What’s the problem?
CustomerMy chicken pasta is cold and the chicken looks a little undercooked.
WaiterI’m very sorry about that. That shouldn’t happen. Let me take it back to the kitchen.
CustomerThank you.
WaiterWould you like the same dish again or something different?
CustomerThe same dish is fine, but please make sure it’s cooked well.
WaiterNo problem. I’ll tell the chef. Thank you for your patience.
CustomerThanks for helping.
WaiterWhile you wait, can I offer you a free drink? Maybe a soft drink or some water?
CustomerA glass of water would be great. Thank you.
WaiterOf course. I’ll bring that right away.
WaiterHere is your new chicken pasta. Could you check if it’s okay now?
CustomerYes, it’s hot and tastes good now.
WaiterWonderful. We also removed it from your bill.
CustomerOh, thank you very much.
WaiterYou’re welcome. Enjoy the rest of your meal.
