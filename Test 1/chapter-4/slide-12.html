SLIDE 12 — NEW LANGUAGE

Apologies in a Restaurant

Complaints & Resolutions

1. APOLOGIZING

The staff apologizes for the problem.

"I'm very sorry about that."

Useful Expressions

  • • I'm very sorry for the inconvenience.
  • • I apologize for the mistake.
  • • That's our fault.
  • • I'm sorry we didn't meet your expectations.
  • • Please accept our apologies.
Good practice: apologize sincerely and take responsibility.
2. ACCEPTING

The customer accepts the apology.

"That's okay, thank you."

Useful Expressions

  • • That's okay.
  • • Thank you.
  • • I appreciate your apology.
  • • It's alright.
  • • No worries.
Good practice: accept with kindness and keep it positive.
3. REFUSING

The customer is not satisfied yet.

"I'm still not satisfied with this."

Useful Expressions

  • • I'm still not happy with this.
  • • That's not acceptable.
  • • I don't think that's enough.
  • • I would like a better solution.
  • • I'm disappointed.
Good practice: listen carefully and offer a better solution.

Example Scenarios

SCENARIO 1 · ACCEPTING

"I'm sorry, your soup is cold. I'll replace it right away."

"That's okay, thank you. I appreciate it."

The staff apologized and fixed the issue — the customer accepted.
SCENARIO 2 · REFUSING

"I apologize for the long wait. The kitchen was very busy."

"I understand, but I've been waiting 45 minutes. I'm still not satisfied."

Listen to the customer and offer another solution (discount, free item, faster service).
